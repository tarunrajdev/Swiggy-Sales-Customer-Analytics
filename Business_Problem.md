#  SWIGGY SALES ANALYSIS - BUSINESS PROBLEM STATEMENT

##  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Swiggy, one of India's leading food delivery platforms, operates in a highly competitive market with over 1,000 restaurant partners across 10 major cities. Despite achieving ₹39.61 Crores in revenue over the past 4 years (2022-2025), the company faces critical challenges in optimizing operations, improving customer retention, and maximizing profitability.

This comprehensive data analysis project aims to uncover actionable insights from 600,000+ orders to drive strategic decision-making across multiple business functions.

---

##  BACKGROUND

### Company Overview

**Swiggy** is a food ordering and delivery platform connecting customers with restaurants across India. The platform operates on a commission-based model, earning revenue from:

- Restaurant commissions (15-25% per order)
- Delivery fees charged to customers
- Premium subscription services
- Advertising and promotional placements

### Current Situation

Over the past 4 years, Swiggy has:

- Served **10,000+ active customers**
- Partnered with **1,000 restaurants** across 10 cities
- Processed **600,000 orders**
- Generated **₹39.61 Crores** in gross order value
- Maintained a **95% order fulfillment rate**

Despite this growth, several challenges have emerged that require data-driven solutions.

---

##  KEY BUSINESS CHALLENGES

### 1. CUSTOMER RETENTION & ENGAGEMENT

**Problem:**

- High customer acquisition costs (CAC) are eating into profit margins
- Customer churn rate is unknown and potentially high
- Lack of understanding about customer lifetime value (CLV)
- No clear segmentation strategy for targeted marketing

**Questions to Answer:**

- What is our customer retention rate?
- Who are our most valuable customers?
- How can we identify customers at risk of churning?
- What drives customer loyalty and repeat purchases?
- Which customer segments should we prioritize?

### 2. REVENUE OPTIMIZATION

**Problem:**

- Revenue growth has plateaued in certain months/cities
- Unclear understanding of revenue drivers
- Seasonal patterns not being leveraged effectively
- Pricing strategy lacks data backing

**Questions to Answer:**

- What are the key revenue drivers (city, cuisine, time, customer segment)?
- Which months/quarters show the highest revenue? Why?
- What is the year-over-year growth rate?
- How does average order value (AOV) vary across segments?
- What pricing strategies can maximize revenue without losing customers?

### 3. RESTAURANT PARTNER PERFORMANCE

**Problem:**

- Significant variance in restaurant performance
- Underperforming restaurants affecting platform reputation
- Unclear criteria for onboarding new restaurant partners
- No systematic approach to restaurant optimization

**Questions to Answer:**

- Which restaurants generate the most revenue?
- How does restaurant rating impact order volume?
- What is the performance difference between cloud kitchens and traditional restaurants?
- Which cuisines are most popular in which cities?
- Which restaurants should we prioritize for marketing investments?
- Which underperforming restaurants need support or should be removed?

### 4. MENU & PRODUCT OPTIMIZATION

**Problem:**

- No clear understanding of menu item performance
- Vegetarian vs non-vegetarian preference patterns unknown
- Pricing strategy for different categories not optimized
- Cross-selling and upselling opportunities not identified

**Questions to Answer:**

- What are the best-selling menu items overall and by category?
- How does vegetarian vs non-vegetarian preference vary by city/demographic?
- What is the optimal price range for different menu categories?
- Which items are frequently ordered together (basket analysis)?
- What menu items drive the highest margins?

### 5. OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY

**Problem:**

- 5% order cancellation rate causing revenue loss and customer dissatisfaction
- Suboptimal delivery capacity during peak hours
- Resource allocation not aligned with demand patterns
- Unclear understanding of operational bottlenecks

**Questions to Answer:**

- What is the hourly/daily order distribution pattern?
- When are the peak ordering times? Are we adequately staffed?
- What is the order cancellation rate by city/time/restaurant?
- Why are orders being cancelled?
- How can we optimize delivery operations?
- What is the weekend vs weekday demand difference?

### 6. MARKET EXPANSION & GROWTH

**Problem:**

- Unclear which cities/areas to prioritize for expansion
- Market saturation in some cities vs untapped potential in others
- No data-driven approach to restaurant onboarding
- Competitive landscape not well understood

**Questions to Answer:**

- Which cities show the highest revenue per restaurant?
- Which cities have room for more restaurant partners?
- What is the optimal restaurant density per city?
- Which cuisines are underrepresented in which cities?
- Where should we expand next?

### 7. CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE & SATISFACTION

**Problem:**

- No systematic analysis of customer behavior patterns
- Payment method preferences not being leveraged
- Demographic preferences not understood
- Personalization opportunities not identified

**Questions to Answer:**

- What are the preferred payment methods by demographic?
- How do different age groups/occupations behave differently?
- What factors influence order value?
- How can we personalize the customer experience?
- What drives customer satisfaction vs dissatisfaction?

### Primary Objectives

**1. Develop Customer Segmentation Strategy**

- Calculate customer lifetime value
- Develop targeted retention strategies

**2. Optimize Revenue Generation**
- Identify top revenue drivers
- Develop pricing optimization recommendations
- Create seasonal marketing strategies
- Maximize average order value

**3. Improve Operational Efficiency**
- Reduce order cancellation rate
- Optimize delivery capacity allocation
- Improve demand forecasting
- Streamline restaurant operations

**4. Enhance Restaurant Partner Ecosystem**
- Identify high-performing restaurant characteristics
- Develop restaurant performance scoring
- Create restaurant onboarding criteria
- Optimize restaurant mix by city

**5. Drive Strategic Growth**
- Identify expansion opportunities
- Develop market penetration strategies
- Create competitive positioning
- Enable data-driven decision making
